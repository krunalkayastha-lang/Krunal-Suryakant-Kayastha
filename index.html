01 — ABOUT

Career summary

Krunal Kayastha is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Parul Institute of Engineering & Technology (Diploma Studies), Vadodara, where he teaches core thermal and mechanical engineering subjects to diploma-level students.

He holds a Master of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering with a specialization in Thermal Engineering from Parul Institute of Engineering & Technology (Gujarat Technological University), and a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from Marudhar Engineering College (Rajasthan University).

His postgraduate research applied CFX-based computational fluid dynamics analysis to automobile radiator design, and he continues to bring that analytical, simulation-driven approach into his teaching and mentorship of engineering students.
